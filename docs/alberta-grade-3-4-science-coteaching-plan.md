**Curriculum source:** Alberta Education new K–6 Science (finalized March 2023)  
**Official curriculum:** [new LearnAlberta – Science](https://curriculum.learnalberta.ca/curriculum/en/s/sci?s=SCI)

## 1. How the two grades are organized

Both grades use the **same seven organizing ideas**. That is the main reason a split 3/4 class can share many lessons.

| Organizing idea | Grade 3 focus (snapshot) | Grade 4 focus (snapshot) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Matter** | How substances change, including water & the water cycle | Waste management & environmental impacts |
| **Energy** | Contact forces & simple machines | Non-contact forces: gravity & magnetism |
| **Earth Systems** | Changes to Earth’s surface; layers/fossils; FNMI land knowledge | Earth’s systems (land, air, water, organisms) & conservation |
| **Living Systems** | Interactions among plants, humans, animals & environment | External structures & sensory organs related to function |
| **Space** | *Not a Grade 3 organizing focus* | Objects in space connected to daily life |
| **Computer Science** | Creativity + computational / divergent thinking | Design processes to solve problems |
| **Scientific Methods** | Sources, accuracy & analysis of data | Role of data & evidence in science |

**Bottom line:** You can teach **shared “big ideas”** together, then use **grade-specific exit tickets, journals, and projects** so each grade still hits its learner outcome.

## 2. Side-by-side similarity map (what you can co-teach)

### Strongest co-teach overlaps (plan these as joint units)

| Shared theme | Grade 3 outcome angle | Grade 4 outcome angle | Split-class strategy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Water & Earth’s systems** | Water cycle; states of water; local water safety | Hydrosphere + how water connects land, air & life; conservation | Shared mini water-cycle experiments; Gr 3 diagrams states/cycle; Gr 4 maps water into Earth spheres & conservation actions |
| **Human impact / stewardship** | Human, plant & animal activities change Earth’s surface; farming in Alberta; respect for land | Waste management; conservation practices; reduce/reuse/recycle/repurpose/repair/compost | Shared “Our impact” inquiry; Gr 3 focus on land change & farming; Gr 4 focus on waste audit + conservation plan |
| **Forces (push/pull family)** | Contact forces; friction; simple machines (lever, wheel, inclined plane); FNMI simple machines | Gravity & magnetism as non-contact forces; poles attract/repel | Shared force language (push/pull, strength, direction); Gr 3 machine stations; Gr 4 magnet/gravity stations |
| **Living things & environments** | Food chains; human effects on plants/animals; protection; FNMI relationships | External structures → function; sensory organs; sensing for survival | Shared local organism study walk; Gr 3 food-chain & impact work; Gr 4 structure–function & senses work |
| **Inquiry / data / design** | Data sources & accuracy; creativity & computational thinking | Evidence in science; design process | Weave into every unit: same investigation, different analysis depth |

### Partial overlaps (shared launch, then diverge)

| Theme | Shared launch | Grade-specific finish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Matter / materials** | Properties of materials; natural vs processed | Gr 3: reversible/permanent change, melting/freezing/boiling, water cycle. Gr 4: hazardous materials symbols, waste methods, personal waste plan |
| **Earth’s surface stories** | Alberta landscapes, erosion stories, land changes | Gr 3: fossils, layers, dinosaurs in Alberta, FNMI intergenerational land knowledge. Gr 4: spheres (litho/hydro/atmo/bio) & how they sustain life |
| **Computer Science** | Creative problem solving with steps/instructions | Gr 3: creative + divergent thinking tasks. Gr 4: full design-process cycle (need → plan → prototype → test → improve) |

### Teach separately (do not force together)

| Grade 3-only priority | Grade 4-only priority |
| --- | --- |
| Simple machines design challenge | Space & daily life (day/night, seasons links, sky objects) |
| Deep dive: fossils / paleontology in Alberta | Magnetism investigations (poles, magnetizing materials) |
| Contact-force investigations with machines | Formal waste-management / dangerous-materials unit |

**Practical tip for a 3/4 split:** When one grade has a unique topic (e.g. Gr 4 Space), run **workshop rotation**: one grade with you on the unique outcome, the other on an independent inquiry, reading, or coding/design task tied to Scientific Methods / Computer Science.

## 3. Suggested year sequence for a 3/4 combined class

Use this as a flexible year map (adjust for your school calendar and field trips).

| Term block | Joint theme | Grade 3 must-hit | Grade 4 must-hit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | Matter + Scientific Methods | Changes of state; water cycle | Waste & dangerous materials |
| **2** | Energy (Forces) + CS design | Contact forces; simple machines | Gravity & magnetism |
| **3** | Earth Systems + Living Systems | Surface change; fossils; interactions | Earth spheres; conservation; structures & senses |
| **4** | Capstone + Grade 4 Space | Integrated inquiry fair | Space & daily life + evidence project |

Weave **Scientific Methods** and **Computer Science** through all blocks rather than isolating them as short “units.”

## 4. Co-teachable unit kits (activities, videos, books)

### Unit A — Water, change, and Earth’s systems  
**Gr 3:** substances change / water cycle · **Gr 4:** water as part of Earth systems + conservation

**In-class activities**
- Bag / jar mini water cycle (warm water + ice lid) — both grades observe; Gr 3 labels evaporation/condensation/precipitation; Gr 4 links to hydrosphere + why conservation matters.
- Alberta seasonal water walk: ice, snow, puddles, runoff — discuss safety around ice (Gr 3 curriculum).
- Conservation action plan: classroom water / litter audit (stronger Gr 4 product; Gr 3 contributes observations).

### Unit C — Forces: contact machines + gravity/magnets  
**Gr 3:** contact forces & simple machines · **Gr 4:** gravity & magnetism

**In-class activities (station day model)**
1. Push/pull & friction stations (both grades).
2. Lever / ramp / wheel challenges (Gr 3 required product: design a device using simple machines).

### Unit D — Living systems: interactions + structures  
**Gr 3:** how plants/animals interact · **Gr 4:** external structures & sensory organs

- Structure–function cards: claw/beak/leaf/root/shell → job (Gr 4 focus; Gr 3 can sort “helps survival how?”).

## 6. Differentiation checklist (so both grades stay compliant)

For **every joint lesson**, prepare:

- [ ] One shared hook / investigation  
- [ ] Grade 3 success criteria tied to *their* learner outcome  
- [ ] Grade 4 success criteria tied to *their* learner outcome  
- [ ] Two exit products (can be short) — e.g. Gr 3 labelled diagram; Gr 4 explanation with evidence  
- [ ] One FNMI / local Alberta connection where curriculum names it (land, water, materials, conservation, machines)  
- [ ] Scientific Methods skill (observe → record → compare sources → conclude)  
- [ ] Optional CS extension (sequence instructions, design loop, Scratch/unplugged coding)

---

## 7. Quick “teach both grades today” lesson pattern

1. **Hook (5–10 min):** video or object demo for both  
2. **Shared investigation (20–25 min):** same materials  
3. **Split processing (15–20 min):** grade-specific notebook prompt / centre  
4. **Share-out (5–10 min):** mixed pairs teach one new idea to the other grade  
5. **Assessment:** sticky-note exit ticket with grade-coded question

This pattern keeps one prep load while protecting curriculum fidelity.
